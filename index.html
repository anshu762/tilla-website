A living tradition

Preserving Punjab's living heritage

Tilla embroidery traces back generations in Amritsar, where artisans once wove gold and silver thread into royal garments. Today, the same karchobi frame and hook technique survive in a handful of family workshops, passed down not through manuals but through years spent watching a parent's hands.

As machine embroidery has taken over the wider market, many of these artisans have been pushed to the margins, their skill undervalued and underpaid. Tilla Threads exists to change that equation, connecting the makers directly to the people who value what they make.
